The audio system is a world system, so playback survives scene changes. Sounds are
attached to actors through an audio source component and controlled through the audio API.

## What this page will cover

- Registering the audio system
- The audio source component
- Playing, stopping and looping
- Volume and mixing
- Working with audio assets
- Browser autoplay restrictions
