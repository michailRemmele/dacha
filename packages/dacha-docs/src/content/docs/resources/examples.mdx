The fastest way to see how the pieces fit together is a finished game. Both of these were
built in a game jam, both are playable in the browser, and the source of both is available.

<GameShowcase
  title="Drillers' Escape"
  jam="GMTK 2026"
  description="Escape a collapsing planet while keeping your fragile mineral cargo intact!"
  play="https://michailremmele.github.io/gmtk-jam-2026/"

/>

What to watch for: the camera tracks the rig continuously, the cave is built from
destructible blocks, and mined cargo attaches to the vehicle and travels with it.

<GameShowcase

What to watch for: dozens of independent actors on screen at once — the school, the fish
that attack it, drifting jellyfish and pickups — spawned and destroyed continuously as the
level runs.
